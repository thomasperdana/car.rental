## 📋 Gig Fleet Rental Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### Phase 1: Driver Screening & Verification

1. **App Approval Check:**
* Require proof of active status on Uber, Lyft, or DoorDash (e.g., screenshot of active driver profile screen with driver name visible).


2. **Identity & ID Check:**
* Collect a clear copy of the driver's License (must be 21+ years old).
* Verify driving record (no major infractions within the past 36 months).


3. **Payment & Deposit Authorization:**
* Secure a refundable deposit (typically $250–$500) via credit or debit card.
* Set up automated recurring weekly billing for rental fees.



---

### Phase 2: Vehicle Prep & Inspection Workflow

1. **Pre-Handout Digital Inspection:**
* Perform a 360-degree walkaround video/photo check using a fleet management app (e.g., Fleetio or Driveroo) or photo timestamp app.
* Document existing scratches, tire tread depth, interior condition, and fuel level/battery charge.


2. **Equipment Check:**
* Ensure OBD-II GPS tracker is securely plugged in and transmitting signal.
* Verify dual dash cam is powered and recording to SD card/cloud.
* Place vehicle key in the window lockbox and set a custom 4-digit PIN for the renter.



---

### Phase 3: Contactless Pick-Up Process

1. **Send Pick-Up Instructions:**
* Text/email the renter 1 hour before scheduled pick-up with:
* Exact vehicle location / GPS pin.
* Vehicle license plate and color.
* Lockbox location and 4-digit PIN.




2. **Renter Self-Inspection (Mandatory):**
* Require the driver to upload 4 photos (front, back, left, right) and odometer reading via your rental app/booking link prior to unlocking the vehicle.



---

### Phase 4: In-Rental Fleet Monitoring & Rules

1. **Geofence & Speed Monitoring:**
* Set automated alerts on your OBD-II app for excessive speeding (>80 MPH) or leaving approved operating zones.


2. **Maintenance Notifications:**
* Monitor engine trouble codes remotely via OBD-II dashboard. Schedule oil changes/maintenance every 5,000 miles during driver downtime.



---

### Phase 5: Return & Deposit Clearance

1. **Contactless Drop-off:**
* Driver parks vehicle at designated hub, locks key back in window lockbox, and submits return photos (exterior, interior, fuel level).


2. **Post-Rental Damage Audit:**
* Inspect car within 2 hours of return against pre-rental baseline.
* Check for unpaid tolls, red-light camera tickets, or cleaning fees (e.g., smoking/spills).


3. **Deposit Release:**
* Process refundable deposit release within 3–5 business days minus any tolls or damages incurred.
