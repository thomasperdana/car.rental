To build a rental fleet optimized for gig workers (like Uber, Lyft, and DoorDash drivers), you need sourcing platforms that offer **reliable, fuel-efficient compacts and hybrids** at wholesale or fair market prices.

---

### 1. Government, Bank, and Fleet Wholesale Auctions

Public and dealer-facing auctions are often the most cost-effective way to buy late-model sedans and hybrids in bulk.

* **GovDeals & PublicSurplus:** These sites auction off retired government, municipal, and police fleet vehicles. You can frequently find well-maintained Toyota Priuses, Ford Fusion Hybrids, and Chevrolet Volts with strict maintenance histories.
* **Capital Auto Auction:** Hosts weekly online auctions featuring fleet turn-ins, bank repossessions, and dealer trade-ins. They offer clean-title vehicles at steep discounts below retail pricing.
* **Copart & IAAI (Clean Title Wholesale Lanes):** While known for salvage cars, both platforms have dedicated "Clean Title" and "Fleet/Lease Return" lanes. Look specifically for "Green Light" cars from financial institutions or rental companies, which often include vehicle condition reports and arbitration policies.

---

### 2. Off-Lease & Former Rental Inventory

Ex-rental and off-lease vehicles are ideal for rental operations because they have documented service histories and are already formatted for commercial use.

* **Enterprise Car Sales:** Enterprise sells its retired rental fleet directly to buyers. Their inventory is rich in high-MPG compacts (Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Elantra, Nissan Versa) and hybrids that are certified and ready for immediate deployment.
* **Hertz Car Sales & Avis Car Sales:** Similar to Enterprise, these major rental agencies sell off late-model compact sedans at competitive, no-haggle prices, often including short-term powertrain warranties.

---

### 3. Commercial Dealer Platforms (Require Dealer License)

If you establish a used-car dealer license or partner with a licensed broker, these platforms unlock wholesale dealer pricing:

* **ACV Auctions:** A leading digital dealer-to-dealer auction platform. ACV provides deep, engine-sound-recorded, and underside-scanned condition reports so you can buy reliable budget hybrids remotely without visiting a physical lot.
* **OPENLANE (formerly ADESA):** A major dealer auction network with extensive inventory in regional hubs. It provides access to off-lease returns directly from manufacturer finance arms (e.g., Toyota Financial, Honda Financial).

---

### 4. Consumer-to-Consumer Marketplaces (Best for Older, Budget Hybrids)

For lower-capital entry (e.g., $5,000–$10,000 older Priuses or Civics), direct private-party platforms offer great cash deals:

* **Facebook Marketplace & Craigslist:** Filter by owner-only listings to find older Toyota Priuses (2010–2015 generation), Honda Civics, and Ford C-Max Hybrids.
* **Autotrader & CarGurus:** Use these aggregators to set alerts for high-MPG sedans priced below market value across both independent dealerships and private sellers.

---

### Sourcing Tips for Rental Fleets

* **Target 2012+ Models:** Most rideshare platforms (Uber/Lyft) enforce vehicle age requirements (typically 15 years old or newer, varying by city/state), whereas delivery services (DoorDash/Instacart) have much looser vehicle age limits.
* **Check Battery Health on Used Hybrids:** When buying pre-owned Priuses or Honda Hybrids, factor in the cost of a refurbished hybrid battery ($1,000–$1,800) if the vehicle has over 150,000 miles.
* **Maintain Pre-Purchase Inspections:** Use mobile inspection services (like *Pomcar* or *Lemon Squad*) before bidding on auction cars or buying from private sellers.
