Here is a comprehensive **10-Point Pre-Purchase Inspection Checklist** designed specifically for fleet operators purchasing vehicles for rideshare and gig delivery drivers.

You can hand this directly to your mobile mechanic before they evaluate any vehicle.

---

## 🚗 Gig Fleet Pre-Purchase Inspection Checklist

### 1. Hybrid System & Battery Health *(Critical for Priuses / Hybrids)*

* [ ] **OBD-II Diagnostic Scan:** Run a full-system scan for pending or historical codes (specifically checking for DTC `P0A80` - Replace Hybrid Battery Pack).
* [ ] **Block Voltage Delta Check:** Check voltage differential across battery blocks under load (delta should ideally be under 0.2V).
* [ ] **Hybrid Cooling Fan Inspection:** Verify the traction battery cooling fan/vent is clean and free of dust or pet hair build-up (a primary cause of battery overheating).

### 2. Engine & Powertrain

* [ ] **Fluid Condition & Leaks:** Inspect engine oil, transmission fluid, and coolant for signs of contamination, head gasket failure (milky oil), or leaks.
* [ ] **Cold Start Test:** Check for abnormal engine knocks, timing chain rattle, or severe vibration on initial startup.
* [ ] **Transmission Engagement:** Confirm smooth shifting with no slipping, hesitations, or clunks when engaging Drive/Reverse.

### 3. Brake System & Regenerative Braking

* [ ] **Pads & Rotors:** Check pad thickness (minimum 5mm recommended) and rotor condition.
* [ ] **Brake Actuator / ABS Module:** Test brake pedal feel for softness or continuous pump cycling (a high-cost repair on older Toyotas).

### 4. Suspension, Steering, & Alignment

* [ ] **Struts & Shocks:** Check for hydraulic fluid leaks or excessive bounce (gig vehicles carry heavy weight and high mileage daily).
* [ ] **Bushings & Control Arms:** Inspect front lower control arm bushings and ball joints for cracking or play.
* [ ] **Tire Wear Uniformity:** Verify tread depth and check for uneven wear, which indicates suspension sagging or bad alignment.

### 5. Tires & Wheels

* [ ] **Tire Age & Brand:** Check the DOT manufacture date code on all 4 tires (tires older than 5 years should be budgeted for replacement).
* [ ] **Matching Tread/Brands:** Verify matched tire specs across axles for optimal fuel efficiency and safety.

### 6. Electrical System & Accessories

* [ ] **12V Auxiliary Battery Test:** Test 12V battery health and alternator voltage output under load (headlights, AC, rear defrost on).
* [ ] **Starter & Ignition:** Ensure clean ignition turns without prolonged cranking.

### 7. Climate Control (HVAC)

* [ ] **A/C Compressor & Cooling:** Verify air conditioning blows ice-cold within 60 seconds (essential for passenger comfort ratings in Uber/Lyft).
* [ ] **Heater Core & Fan Speeds:** Confirm all blower speeds and temperature mixing function properly.

### 8. Body, Frame, & Undercarriage

* [ ] **Structural / Frame Integrity:** Inspect frame rails, unibody seams, and floor pans for rust, bends, or repair welds.
* [ ] **Panels & Gaps:** Check body panel alignment and paint consistency for signs of unreported minor accidents.
* [ ] **Underbody Shields:** Ensure plastic engine splash guards and aerodynamic belly pans are present and secure.

### 9. Cabin & Door Mechanics

* [ ] **Seat & Belt Mechanics:** Test driver and passenger seat adjustments, seatbelts, and latch mechanisms.
* [ ] **Windows & Locks:** Test all power window regulators, door locks, and interior door handles from both inside and outside.

### 10. Road Test Evaluation

* [ ] **Highway Cruise (50–70 MPH):** Check for high-speed wheel vibration, wind noise, or pulling to one side.
* [ ] **Tight Turning Radius:** Listen for CV axle clicking or power steering whine during full-lock low-speed turns.
* [ ] **Real-Time MPG Monitor:** Confirm the instrument cluster shows expected fuel economy baseline during testing.

---

### Pro-Tip for Fleet Buyers

Always ask the mechanic to assign each item a **Pass / Monitor / Immediate Repair** rating along with estimated repair costs. You can use these estimated repair costs as direct leverage to negotiate down the purchase price with the seller!
