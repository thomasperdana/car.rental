When outfitting rental vehicles for gig drivers (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash), your primary equipment goals are **security, automated handoffs, accountability, and fleet durability**.

Here is the essential gear every car in your rental fleet should have, along with where to get it and top product recommendations.

---

### 1. Key Lockboxes for Contactless Pickups & Drop-offs

Contactless key transfers allow drivers to pick up or drop off cars 24/7 without needing your direct physical presence. Window-mounted combination lockboxes secure the key fob directly onto the vehicle.

* **KeyGuard Pro Car Window Lockbox:** Designed specifically for rental fleets, with punch-button access and a rubber-backed housing that prevents scratching vehicle glass or paint.

The KeyGuard Pro SL-591-CVR Car Window Key Box securely hangs on a vehicle window with a durable, weather-resistant keypad cover.

* **Where to Buy:** Available via major retailers including Walmart, eBay, and direct from **KeyGuard** or **Amazon**.

---

### 2. OBD-II GPS Trackers with Remote Diagnostics

A plugged-in 4G OBD-II tracker provides real-time location tracking, geofencing, speed alerts, and engine trouble code monitoring (DTCs) to ensure your renters stay within agreed regions and do not abuse the vehicle.

* **AccuGPS OBD II Smart GPS Tracker:** Offers real-time location monitoring, mileage reports, diagnostic fault codes, and driver behavior scores.

The AccuGPS OBD II GPS Tracker for Vehicles plugs directly into the vehicle's OBD-II port for location updates, trip histories, and vehicle health alerts.

* **ShadowGPS OBD-II Tracker:** Plugs into any OBD port, tracking vehicle location, speed alerts, and tamper notifications directly through a dedicated mobile app.

The ShadowGPS GPS Tracker for Vehicles gives fleet owners real-time location, speed notifications, and diagnostic reports with simple setup.

* **Where to Buy:** Direct from manufacturers like **AccuGPS**, **ShadowGPS**, **LiveViewGPS**, or commercial fleet platforms like **Samsara** and **BouncingGPS**.

---

### 3. Commercial Dual Dash Cams (Road & Cabin Facing)

Dual dash cameras protect your liability in accidents and record cabin footage in case of disputes. High-end fleet units automatically save video to the cloud when high G-force events (collisions or hard braking) are detected.

* **RastracVision AI Dashboard Camera Bundle:** A full commercial fleet setup featuring road- and cabin-facing cameras, AI safety alerts, and integrated GPS fleet monitoring.

The RastracVision Dashboard Camera Bundle offers dual HD camera coverage with built-in AI alerts for hard braking, speeding, and incident resolution.

* **VIOFO Dual & 3-Channel Cameras:** Offers 4K road capture and interior cabin recording with night vision for independent rental owners seeking reliable local SD card recording.

The VIOFO A329WW 3CH 4K Dash Cam features multi-channel recording for front, cabin, and rear protection.

* **Where to Buy:** **VIOFO**, **EzDashcam**, **Tavco Technologies**, or fleet security suppliers like **Nexar Fleet** and **BlackVue**.

---

### 4. Additional Fleet Durability Accessories

To protect your vehicle interiors from daily delivery wear-and-tear:

* **All-Weather Rubber Floor Mats (WeatherTech or Husky Liners):** Protects carpet from dirt, spills, and heavy wear from delivery drivers getting in and out constantly.
* **Heavy-Duty Seat Covers:** Waterproof neoprene or faux-leather seat covers preserve factory upholstery against food or drink spills.
* **MagSafe / Universal Commercial Phone Mounts:** Providing a heavy-duty vent or dash phone mount discourages drivers from holding phones while driving, keeping your vehicles safer.
